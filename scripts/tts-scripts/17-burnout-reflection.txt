Spent some time this evening trying to honestly assess where I'm at with work, and I think I've been creeping toward burnout for a while without really naming it. The signs have been there for months: waking up already tired, dreading things I used to find interesting, going through the motions on projects that should excite me. I've been telling myself it's just a busy period and things will ease up, but busy periods don't usually last eight months. The thing that's hard about burnout is that it doesn't hit all at once. It's this gradual erosion where you don't notice how depleted you are until you try to do something that requires real energy and realize you have nothing to give. I had a conversation with a friend last week who said something that stuck with me, that rest isn't the reward for finishing your work, it's the condition required to do work well. I've been treating recovery as optional, something I'll get to eventually, and that's clearly not working. Going to talk to my manager about reducing scope for the next quarter and actually take the holiday I've been pushing back. I need to treat this seriously before it gets worse.
