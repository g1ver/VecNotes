Went out for a four-hour ride today, about ninety kilometres through the hills north of town. The first climb absolutely destroyed me, which is embarrassing because it's not even that steep, maybe six percent average gradient for four kilometres, but my legs just weren't there yet. Things came good around the halfway point though and I felt strong for the back half. Power numbers were decent, normalized power around two thirty watts which is about where I want to be for a ride that long. The descent on the back road was sketchy because there had been some rain overnight and there were patches of gravel washed across the road that I nearly came off on twice. Need to be more careful about checking conditions before heading out. Ate two rice cakes, a banana, and two gels over the four hours, which was probably not quite enough because I was running low on energy in the final thirty minutes. Hydration was good though, got through about two litres. The bike itself needs a chain clean and the rear derailleur is making a noise that I should look at before next weekend. All up a good training day even with the rough start.
