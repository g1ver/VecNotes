Did my longest training run yet this morning, eighteen miles out along the canal path and back through the park. Legs felt surprisingly good through mile twelve, then the familiar tightness started creeping into my left hip flexor, which has been a recurring issue since I ramped up mileage three weeks ago. I slowed down to about a nine-thirty pace for the last four miles instead of pushing through, and I think that was the right call. Nutrition-wise I took a gel at mile six and mile twelve and had a small bottle of electrolytes which seemed to be the right amount. Heart rate averaged about one forty-two which is solidly in zone two, exactly where I want to be for these long slow distance runs. The race is eight weeks out now. Going to book a physio appointment this week to look at the hip before it becomes a real problem, and I want to start incorporating more hip flexor stretching into my morning routine. Overall pretty happy with where fitness is sitting right now.
