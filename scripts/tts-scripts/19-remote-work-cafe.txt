Worked from a café today for the first time in a while and was reminded of how much location affects my ability to concentrate. There's something about being in a semi-public space with ambient noise that puts me in a different mode than working from home. At home I have too many easy escapes, the couch, the kitchen, things that need doing around the flat. In a café there's really only one thing to do and that's work. The background noise is actually helpful for a certain type of task, writing, planning, lighter thinking work. The deep technical stuff I still prefer to do in proper silence, but for everything else the café is often better. Found a place about fifteen minutes from home that has good wifi, strong coffee, and doesn't feel rushed even if you're there for a few hours. It's quiet enough on a Tuesday morning that you can actually think. The main downside is that calls are impossible without stepping outside, which means I had to reschedule one today. Should check my calendar before committing to café days and make sure there's nothing with audio. Going to make this a regular Tuesday thing and see if it helps with the general work from home fatigue I've been feeling.
