Finished tiling the bathroom floor this weekend and it looks better than I expected given it was my first time doing any kind of tile work. The preparation took most of Saturday: checking the floor was level, using self-levelling compound on a low spot near the door, letting it cure overnight, and then marking out the layout so the cuts at the walls were even on both sides rather than ending up with a sliver on one end. The actual tiling went faster than I expected once I had a rhythm. The tricky part was cutting the tiles around the toilet base which required a jigsaw and a tile blade rather than the straight cuts the rest of the floor needed. Grout is where I struggled, I applied it too wet the first time and it sagged and was hard to clean off the tile faces before it set. Scraped it out and redid that section with a stiffer mix the following morning. The finished floor has one grout line that's slightly wider than the others which I can see every time I look for it and probably nobody else will ever notice. Total cost in materials was about ninety pounds versus the six hundred I was quoted by a tiler. Took longer but I learned a genuinely useful skill and I have a much better sense now of what I can tackle myself versus what needs a professional. Wet room waterproofing, for example, I'll leave to someone with experience.
