Started seriously planning the Japan trip tonight and two weeks is starting to feel very short for a country this large and varied. The classic dilemma seems to be whether to go deep in fewer places or cover more ground and see more. Tokyo alone could absorb a week without effort, and then there's Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, and the question of whether to go north toward Tohoku or southwest toward Hiroshima. Leaning toward a Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka core with a day in Nara and maybe one day trip to Hakone for Fuji views if the weather cooperates. The rail pass question is more complicated than I expected, the 14-day JR pass is expensive and only makes financial sense if you're doing a lot of long-distance bullet train travel. Given the route I'm planning it probably does make sense. Food is a big part of why I want to go and I've been keeping a running list of specific things to eat in specific places: ramen at a counter in Shinjuku, kaiseki in Kyoto, okonomiyaki in Osaka, fresh sushi at Tsukiji or one of the outer market stalls. The language barrier feels manageable based on what I've read, most cities have good English signage and Google Translate has gotten genuinely good at Japanese. Going in early April for cherry blossom season means booking accommodation months in advance which I should stop procrastinating on and do this weekend.
