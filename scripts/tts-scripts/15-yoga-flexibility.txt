Finally committed to a proper yoga practice this week instead of just doing the occasional stretch when something hurts. Went to a ninety-minute yin class last night and it was humbling in the best way. I thought because I'm reasonably fit I would be okay, but flexibility is genuinely its own capability entirely separate from cardiovascular fitness or strength. My hamstrings and hip flexors are almost embarrassingly tight, probably from years of sitting at a desk and running without enough mobility work. The yin format, where you hold poses for three to five minutes, was actually easier for me mentally than I expected. There's something almost meditative about staying in an uncomfortable position and just breathing into it rather than fighting it. Noticed that my lower back, which has been nagging me on and off for months, felt noticeably looser the morning after. The instructor mentioned that fascia takes a long time to respond to stretching, we're talking months of consistent practice rather than weeks, which reset my expectations. Going to do two yin classes a week and add a fifteen-minute morning routine at home to build the habit properly. Already booked the next three sessions.
