Made real progress on the novel today, about fourteen hundred words which is more than I usually manage in a single sitting. I've been struggling with the second act for weeks because I plotted myself into a corner where my protagonist has no obvious reason to stay in the situation driving the conflict. Spent two hours this morning not writing but just thinking through the character's motivations and I think I found the answer: she's not staying because she has to, she's staying because she believes she's the only person who knows the full story and leaving feels like abandoning the people who trusted her. That gives her agency rather than just making her a victim of circumstance, which was the problem with my previous framing. Once I had that clarity the scene I'd been avoiding practically wrote itself. I've been trying to write first drafts with the inner critic turned off as much as possible, accepting that some of what I produce will be bad and trusting revision to fix it. That mindset is genuinely hard for me but when it works the output is so much faster. Three months into this project and I'm at about forty-two thousand words out of a target of eighty thousand. Still a long way to go but the shape of the whole thing is clearer now than it's ever been.
