Hit a wall with my bench press today and it's been frustrating me for about three weeks now. I've been stuck at a hundred kilograms for three sets of five and every time I try to push to a hundred and two point five I fail on the third set. Did some reading tonight and I think the issue might actually be my programming rather than just strength. I've been running the same linear progression scheme for almost six months and my body has probably adapted as much as it can to that stimulus. Thinking about switching to a wave loading approach, something like five sets at different intensities across the week, instead of always grinding at the same weight. Also realized my shoulder warm-up has been pretty lazy lately, just a few arm circles and straight into the work sets. Going to add a proper rotator cuff sequence before I touch the bar from now on. Sometimes the plateau isn't a strength problem, it's a programming and recovery problem. Lesson learned, adjustments start Monday.
