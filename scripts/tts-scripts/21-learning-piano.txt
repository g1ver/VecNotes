Put in another hour on the piano tonight working on the left hand for the Bach Minuet I've been learning. The coordination between hands is genuinely one of the harder things I've tried to learn as an adult. My brain keeps wanting to simplify the left hand pattern whenever the right hand gets complicated, like it can only allocate full attention to one thing at a time. My teacher suggested practicing each hand in isolation until both are completely automatic before trying to put them together, which I've been impatient about, but I tried it seriously tonight and the difference was immediately obvious. Spent forty minutes just drilling the left hand at half tempo until it felt effortless, then put the hands together and the coordination was noticeably easier. There's also the issue of tension in my forearms, I grip the keys much harder than necessary when I'm concentrating, which tires my hands out quickly and probably affects the tone. Trying to stay conscious of keeping my shoulders dropped and my wrists loose, though it's hard to monitor technique when you're also thinking about the notes. Only been playing for four months so I'm trying to be patient, but I can already hear improvement from last month which is motivating. Booked an extra lesson this week to work through the tricky section in bars nine through twelve.
