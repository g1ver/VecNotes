Had the final round interview today for the senior role I've been pursuing and came away feeling cautiously optimistic but not certain. The technical panel went well, the problems were the kind of systems design questions I'd prepared for and I was able to talk through my reasoning clearly without getting flustered. The culture fit conversation was harder to read. I tried to be honest about my working style rather than performing enthusiasm, which I think was the right call but sometimes interviewers want the performance. The question that caught me off guard was about a time I had a significant professional disagreement and how I handled it. I had an answer but it wasn't as clean as I'd have liked, I fumbled the framing and realized halfway through that I was describing a situation where I was probably the difficult one. Tried to recover with honesty about what I'd do differently, which might have landed well or might have been too much self-criticism for an interview context. Hard to tell. The company seems genuinely good, the engineering culture came across as thoughtful and the problems they're working on are interesting. Compensation range is above what I'm making now which matters practically. Expect to hear back within a week. Whatever the outcome I'm going to write down the questions they asked because this kind of preparation sharpens fast and I don't want to forget it.
