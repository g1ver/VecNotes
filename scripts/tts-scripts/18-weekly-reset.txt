Did my Sunday reset this afternoon and it made such a difference to how I feel heading into the week. Spent about two hours going through everything: cleared my inbox down to zero, reviewed all my open tasks and projects, did a grocery shop with an actual meal plan instead of just winging it, tidied the apartment, and laid out my clothes and gym bag for Monday morning. None of these things individually are that significant but doing them all together creates this feeling of having a clean slate, like the week ahead is actually planned rather than just happening to me. I've been inconsistent with this habit over the past few months and the difference is noticeable. The weeks where I don't do a proper reset tend to spiral into reactive firefighting by Wednesday. I also reviewed my goals for the quarter which I haven't looked at in three weeks, and realized I've drifted pretty significantly from what I said I wanted to focus on. Not in a catastrophic way, just the usual drift that happens when you're in the weeds. Recalibrated and picked three things I want to make real progress on before the end of the month. Feels good to have direction again.
