Did a forty-minute breathwork session this morning instead of my usual twenty-minute sit and the difference in the aftermath was notable. The technique was box breathing extended out, four counts in, hold for eight, out for eight, hold for four, repeated in cycles for about thirty minutes before finishing with a five-minute open awareness sit. By the end of the extended exhale phase my body was deeply relaxed in a way that feels physiologically different from just sitting quietly. I've been reading about the mechanics of it and the long exhales activate the parasympathetic nervous system more strongly than slow breathing alone, which is why it can shift your state so quickly. What I find interesting is that breathwork is one of the few practices where you're directly manipulating the autonomic nervous system, which is supposed to be involuntary. That access feels significant. I've been skeptical of a lot of the wellness content around breathwork because it gets tangled up with claims that are hard to evaluate, but the subjective effect on my mental state is real enough to take seriously regardless of the mechanism. Went into a difficult work call an hour later and was noticeably more grounded and less reactive than I usually am. Could be placebo, could be the actual physiology, probably both. Planning to build a longer session into my Wednesday mornings going forward.
