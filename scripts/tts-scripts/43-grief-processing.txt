It's been three months since my uncle died and grief is such a strange non-linear thing. There are days now where I barely think about it and then something completely random, a song he would have liked or someone using one of his phrases, will hit me so hard I have to sit down. I expected grief to proceed in some kind of orderly way, getting gradually better over time, and instead it comes in waves that don't correlate with how much time has passed or how prepared I thought I was. Talked to my cousin last week who was closest to him and she said the same thing, that she can go a whole week feeling okay and then something small completely undoes her. There's comfort in knowing that's normal. What I've found helps is not trying to manage it or hurry through it, just letting the feeling arrive and be present with it rather than distracting myself immediately. I've been writing about him a bit, just memories and things I want to remember, and that has been more valuable than I expected. He was someone who paid real attention to people and I want to carry something of that forward rather than just losing it. The people we love reshape us and part of grief is figuring out what those changes are.
