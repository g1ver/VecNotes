Spent a couple of hours this evening putting together a playlist for a friend's birthday dinner and ended up falling down a rabbit hole of music I'd completely forgotten about. The brief was something that worked as background music for a dinner party, present but not demanding attention, interesting but not jarring when conversation was happening. That's actually a surprisingly specific requirement and harder than putting together a playlist for personal listening. Instrumentals work well, or songs where the vocals blend into the texture rather than demanding you listen to the lyrics. Ended up building around a core of nineties bossa nova covers, a handful of Ahmad Jamal and Bill Evans tracks for the jazz contingent, and some contemporary stuff from artists like Khruangbin and Parcels that sits somewhere between genres in a way that doesn't impose a mood. The sequencing matters more than I usually think about it, the energy arc of a playlist shapes how it feels to be in a room. Started mellow, let it build slightly through the dinner, and brought it back down for dessert. Music taste is intensely personal and making something for other people is always a bit of a guess, but I think this one works. Three hours of material for a dinner that will probably last two. Already have ideas for a follow-up playlist that goes somewhere slightly different.
