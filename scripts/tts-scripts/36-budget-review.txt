Did a proper audit of my finances tonight for the first time in about four months and found a few things I'd been avoiding looking at. Subscriptions have crept up to an embarrassing amount, I'm paying for three streaming services I barely use, a gym membership at a gym I haven't been to since March, and a software tool I switched away from six months ago but forgot to cancel. Cancelled everything unnecessary and that alone saves about eighty pounds a month. The bigger picture is less alarming than I feared. Savings rate is sitting at about twenty-two percent of take-home which is below my target of twenty-five but not catastrophically off. The main drag is eating out, which has somehow doubled since I moved to a new neighborhood with more restaurants nearby. Not interested in cutting it to zero because those meals are genuinely important to my social life, but I'm going to try cooking at home for at least four dinners a week which should bring the number down without feeling like deprivation. Also realized I've been keeping too much cash in a current account earning essentially nothing when I could have it in a high interest savings account. Transferring the surplus this week. These kinds of reviews are uncomfortable to start but they always leave me feeling more in control than before I looked.
