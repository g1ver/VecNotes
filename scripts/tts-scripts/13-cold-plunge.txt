Tried cold water immersion for the first time this morning and it was genuinely one of the more uncomfortable things I've done voluntarily. The plunge pool at the gym is kept at nine degrees Celsius and I managed four minutes before I had to get out. The first thirty seconds were almost panic-inducing, that sharp involuntary gasp reflex is no joke, but once I slowed my breathing down and accepted that I wasn't actually in danger the whole thing became more manageable. By the two-minute mark there was this strange calm that settled in, almost meditative. After I got out and warmed up, my mood was noticeably better for hours, like a baseline elevation that I don't usually feel from normal exercise. I've seen a lot of conflicting research on whether it actually aids muscle recovery or blunts adaptation, so I'm less interested in it for recovery and more for the mental side, the deliberate discomfort tolerance. Planning to do it three mornings a week and track how sleep and mood respond over the next month. Curious to see whether the adaptation makes it easier or whether it's always going to feel like that.
