Recorded an episode today with a guest I've been trying to book for about six months and it went really well, almost three hours of conversation that I'll have to cut down to about sixty minutes for release. The preparation made a real difference. I spent four hours this week reading everything my guest had published in the last two years and listening to every other interview they'd done so I could avoid the questions they've answered dozens of times and go somewhere more interesting. The best conversations happen when the guest feels like they're saying something new rather than reciting a familiar narrative. There's a moment in a good interview where you can almost see them lean in because you've asked about something they haven't been asked before. Got that twice today, once when I asked about a project that failed that they'd never spoken publicly about, and once on a topic they'd written about but nobody had followed up on. Audio quality was the usual challenge, I'm recording remotely and there's always a tradeoff between asking people to buy better microphones and not creating friction that makes people say no to being a guest. Going to invest in a better preamp for my end and see if that helps the overall sound quality on the mixed episode. Editing starts tomorrow and I'm hoping to get this one out in about two weeks.
