Spent a few hours this weekend trying to get my personal knowledge system into better shape. I've accumulated notes across three different apps over the past two years, some in Notion, some in Apple Notes, and a bunch of loose text files on my desktop that I've never properly organized. The result is that I've captured a lot of information but I can almost never find it when I need it. Read through some of Tiago Forte's second brain ideas and the thing that resonated most was the distinction between storing information for retrieval versus processing information for understanding. A lot of my note-taking has been passive archiving without the step of putting things in my own words, which means I've collected a lot but internalized very little. Starting a new system where anything worth keeping gets distilled into my own phrasing with a note on why it matters to me. Also moving everything into one tool to stop the fragmentation. It's going to take a few weekends to migrate and organize, but the alternative is continuing to have a digital junk drawer that I never actually use. The goal is a system I trust enough to actually reach for when I need it.
